Squares & Square Roots Guide

1. Perfect Squares (1 to 400)

A perfect square is the product of a whole number multiplied by itself.

Example: 15 × 15 = 15² = 225
Square Root: √225 = 15

2. Estimating Non-Perfect Squares

To estimate square roots like √50:

  1. Find Perfect Squares Around It: 49 (7²) and 64 (8²). So √50 is between 7 and 8.
  2. Estimate the Decimal: 50 is much closer to 49 than 64, so √50 ≈ 7.1.
